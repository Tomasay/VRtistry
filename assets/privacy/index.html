Privacy Policy

VRtistry · Effective October 23, 2026

VRtistry is a party game: one player paints in a Meta Quest headset while everyone else guesses on their phones at vrtistry.app. This policy explains what information the game handles, why, and who else sees it. VRtistry is made by Thomas Ratliff (Synthlab Studios), an independent developer in the United States, who is responsible for this information.

  • VRtistry has no accounts, no ads and no analytics of its own, and never sells your information.
  • What you enter in a game (a nickname, a face doodle, your guesses and drawings) is shared with the other players in that game so the game can be played.
  • We do not use your microphone or camera.

Information we handle

If you play in the headset (Meta Quest)

  • Your name from your Meta account. With your Meta account's permission, the game reads your display name through Meta's Platform SDK and uses the first word of it (or your Meta username if there is no display name) as your default in-game name. You can change it at any time on the in-game mirror. We do not save it; it is read again each time the game starts.
  • Gameplay: the position of your headset and controllers (so your avatar moves for other players), your drawings, your avatar's appearance, and a picture of your in-game avatar's face used on the scoreboard. This picture is drawn by the game; it is not taken with a camera.
  • Settings on your headset, such as volume, movement and comfort options, your last party code (so you can rejoin a game within 15 minutes), and whether you have finished a game. These stay on your headset.
  • The Meta Platform SDK also checks that you own the game (an entitlement check). Meta handles that check under Meta's own privacy policy.

If you play on your phone

  • What you enter: a nickname, a face doodle, a color and hat, your answers and guesses, and your score.
  • A random player ID, made up by the game, so it can recognize your phone if you reconnect. It is not linked to your real identity.
  • Browser storage: your nickname, color, hat and player ID are kept in your browser so they are filled in next time. Your doodle, answers and party code are kept only so you can rejoin a game you dropped out of within 15 minutes, and are cleared when you leave or finish a game. You can remove all of it by clearing this site's data in your browser. We do not use cookies.

What we do not collect

VRtistry does not record your voice, use your microphone or cameras, use passthrough, or track your eyes, face, hands or body (beyond the headset and controller positions above). It does not ask for your email, age, location or contacts in the game.

How we use it

Only to run the game: to show players their names, doodles, drawings and scores, keep everyone's game in sync, and let you reconnect if your connection drops. We do not use your information for advertising or profiling.

Who else sees it

  • The other players in your game. Everyone in a game sees each other's names, doodles, drawings, guesses and scores, and the headset player's name and avatar picture.
  • Normcore (Normal VR, Inc.) runs the servers that connect players in a game and relays the game data above between them. Normcore's servers can see your IP address and process game data in the United States, the Netherlands, Germany, the United Arab Emirates, Singapore, Japan, Australia, South Africa, Brazil and India, depending on which server is closest to the headset player. See Normcore for their terms.
  • Meta provides your name and the ownership check described above, and Meta's software on the headset may send Meta its own diagnostic data under Meta's privacy policy.
  • Unity Technologies, the maker of the game engine, may receive basic, anonymous hardware information about your device (such as its model and operating system) under Unity's privacy policy.
  • Our website host and font provider. Loading vrtistry.app sends your IP address and browser details to the company that hosts the site, and the phone game loads fonts from Google Fonts, which also receives your IP address. Other pages on this site may load scripts or images from content delivery networks (such as jsDelivr and cdnjs), which works the same way.
  • Newsletter. If you choose to sign up for news on our home page, your email address is sent to Mailchimp (Intuit Inc.), which stores it to send you updates. You can unsubscribe from any email.
  • When you share. At the end of a game you can save or share a picture of the results. That picture is made on your phone and includes players' names, doodles and drawings; it only goes where you choose to send it. Please be considerate of the other players when sharing.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long it is kept

VRtistry does not keep game information on its own servers. Players and their doodles are deleted from Normcore's servers when the game ends. Some shared game state, such as scores and the headset player's name, may stay stored with that game's room on Normcore's servers after everyone leaves, until the room code is used again; the game does not use it for anything else. Information kept on your headset or in your browser stays there until you delete the game or clear the site's data.

Children

VRtistry is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Meta Quest accounts have their own age requirements. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices and rights

  • Change your in-game name on the mirror (headset) or the name screen (phone) at any time.
  • Clear your phone's stored VRtistry information by clearing this site's data in your browser, and your headset's by uninstalling the game.
  • Depending on where you live (for example in California or the European Union), you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it. Because VRtistry does not store your information on its own servers, there is usually nothing for us to hand over, but you can contact us with any request or question and we will respond.

Changes to this policy

If we change how VRtistry handles your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
